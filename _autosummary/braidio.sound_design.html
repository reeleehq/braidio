braidio.sound_design#

Sound design for a rendered production: foley chooses and places the effects (braidio#161).

braidio designs no sound of its own beyond the caller’s music bed and the structural stings. Sound effects are foley’s job: foley.score() reads the narration text, picks sounds under its fail-closed licence gate, aligns them to the spoken words in the rendered audio, weaves them in and masters the mix. This module is the one adapter from a braidio render to that call:

  • SoundDesign is the switch and its knobs. Its absence is “off”: a render that is not given one is byte-for-byte what it was before.

  • talk_segments() turns the render’s talk spans (where each spoken beat sits on the episode, and what it says) into foley’s segments: one per sentence, each with an estimated start, so foley’s per-segment max_events cap is per sentence (the taste ceiling) and its aligner searches the right stretch of audio rather than the whole file. Clips and stings contribute no segment, so no effect is anchored to a song excerpt.

  • score_mix() runs the designer over the mix and writes the result.

foley is the optional braidio[sound] extra, imported only when a design runs. The designer is a seam (SoundDesign(designer=...)), so a host can bring its own and the tests run without foley.

>>> talk_segments([TalkSpan(start_s=2.0, duration_s=4.0, text="Rain fell. Door shut.")])
(['Rain fell.', 'Door shut.'], [2.0, 4.0])

class braidio.sound_design.SoundDesign(max_events=6, master='podcast', max_usd=None, allow_generate=True, designer=None, writer=None)[source]#

Bases: object

Sound design is on, with these knobs (foley’s defaults throughout).

max_events caps the sounds per sentence; master is foley’s delivery target (it always masters the woven mix, and "podcast" is -16 LUFS / -1 dBTP, braidio’s own default); max_usd caps what one run may spend (None: foley’s default cap); allow_generate=False is retrieval only, with no paid generation fallback (offline and CI renders). The licence intent is not a knob here: it follows the render’s rights profile (commercial_ok()). designer is the strategy that does the work, called like foley.score() (default: foley itself), and writer the one that writes its mix, (weave, path) -> None (default: soundfile then ffmpeg to MP3); neither is part of the record, and both are fast-path only (the graph runs nw’s foley Transform, which records them).

braidio.sound_design.score_mix(audio, spans, *, design, profile, out_path)[source]#

Sound-design the mix at audio and write the result to out_path.

spans is the talk on the mix’s timeline (talk_segments()). The design’s designer (default foley.score()) is called with foley’s keywords; its writer writes the weave result (default: an MP3 through ffmpeg). out_path may be audio itself: the mix is fully read before anything is written.

Raises ValueError when there is no talk to design for, rather than hand foley nothing and report an undesigned mix as designed.

braidio.sound_design.talk_segments(spans)[source]#

foley’s (segments, segment_starts) for the talk on a timeline.

Each span’s sentences start where the span starts, then advance in proportion to the characters spoken before them. That is an estimate (the beat’s start is exact, the sentence’s is not), and it only has to put foley’s aligner in the right stretch of audio.

foley needs every worded segment to start strictly after the previous one. The weave crossfades consecutive beats, so a beat can start before the previous one ends: each span’s sentences are therefore spread over its window, from its start to the next span’s start (or its own end when that comes first), never over its full duration.

>>> talk_segments([TalkSpan(0.0, 3.0, "One. Two two two."), TalkSpan(5.0, 1.0, "")])
(['One.', 'Two two two.'], [0.0, 0.75])

A long beat whose short last sentence would land after the crossfaded next beat’s start stays inside its window:

>>> talk_segments([TalkSpan(0.0, 12.0, "x" * 98 + ". No."), TalkSpan(11.88, 2.0, "Next.")])[1]
[0.0, 11.531, 11.88]
Return type:

tuple[list[str], list[float]]
